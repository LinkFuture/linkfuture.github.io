Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Link Future LLC ("we", "us") builds UniBucket with a local-first design. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Cloud Credentials and Files

UniBucket requires your own cloud storage account (for example AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, Cloudflare R2, or MinIO). Your access keys and service account files are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage and kept only on your device. File transfers go directly between your device and your cloud provider. We never receive your credentials, your files, or your bucket contents.

Licensing & Purchases

UniBucket includes a 30-day free trial that is tracked locally on your device. When you activate, validate, or deactivate a license, the app sends your license key and an anonymous device identifier to our payment and licensing provider, Lemon Squeezy, to verify the license and enforce device limits. Purchases on Windows and direct downloads are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which collects the payment and contact details you enter at checkout under its own privacy policy. We receive order details such as your email address and license information so we can provide support.

For purchases made through the Mac App Store, transactions are processed directly by Apple via In-App Purchase in accordance with Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment details for Mac App Store transactions.

No Analytics or Tracking

UniBucket does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking. Diagnostic logs stay on your device and are only shared if you choose to send them to us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information, except with Lemon Squeezy as described above or when required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
