FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my 30-day free trial ends?

Your cloud files, buckets, and saved accounts remain completely untouched. Once the 30-day trial expires, simply enter a license key to unlock UniBucket and pick up right where you left off.

Which cloud storage providers are supported?

UniBucket supports AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, and any S3-compatible object storage service — including Cloudflare R2, MinIO, Backblaze B2, DigitalOcean Spaces, Wasabi, and self-hosted S3 endpoints.

Is UniBucket a subscription or a one-time purchase?

UniBucket is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription fees. You can activate your license key directly inside the app on Windows, macOS, or Linux, and deactivate a device anytime if you switch machines.

Are my cloud credentials and files sent to LinkFuture servers?

No. UniBucket is local-first: your credentials are encrypted locally using your operating system's secure storage, and all file transfers happen directly between your computer and your cloud provider. We never see your keys, buckets, or files.
